Activity 5: Oral practice

Read and act out the following dialogue.

Wazo:

Hi, Keli!

Keli:

Hi, Wazo! How are you?

Wazo:

I’m fine. Thank you. You’re in blue today.

Keli:

Why do you say so?

Wazo:

You are in the blue shirt, trousers and shoes. Your book is also blue!

Keli:

Stop joking! I’m also in red socks, and my cap is green.

Wazo:

By the way, did you buy that goose?

Keli:

No. It was too expensive.

Wazo:

Oh, sorry! I’m going to fetch firewood. I have to go.

Keli:

Be careful of snakes.

Wazo:

Don’t worry. Bye!

Keli:

Bye!
