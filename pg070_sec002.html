Maswi, the lucky boy
A long time ago, there was a boy called Maswi. He lived with his parents in a remote village. In the village, there was a forbidden forest. One day, Maswi was walking through the forest. Suddenly, he met five blind lions. The lions were searching for food. They noticed him by smell. They snarled and charged at him. In the blink of an eye, Maswi jumped into the forest. The lions followed him. He shivered and bit his teeth in fear.
