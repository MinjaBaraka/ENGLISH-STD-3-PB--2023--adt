Oral practice

Activity 8: Oral practice

In pairs, take turns to ask and answer the following questions.

(i) Have you ever been to a national park?

(ii) What national parks do you know?

(iii) What wild animals do you know?

(iv) Which animals do you like the most? Why?

(v) What birds do you know?

(vi) Which birds do you like the most? Why?
