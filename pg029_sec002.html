Listen to the dialogue/observe the signed dialogue. Then, act it out in pairs.

Asia: Hello, Tino! Do you have a cat at home? Augustino: Yes, I do. We have also a dog called Simba. Do you have a cat? Asia: Yes, we have a cat called Snow. Augustino: Your cat has a nice name. Does it have blue eyes? Asia: Yes, it has blue eyes and soft white fur. Does your dog have a long tail? Augustino: Yes, it has a long tail. It also has strong legs. Asia: I have a ball for our cat to play with. Does Simba have toys to play with? Augustino: Yes, Simba has a red ball and a rubber bone. My brother has a rope for Simba too. Asia: Your dog has many toys! I have only one toy for our cat. Augustino: That’s okay. Asia: I agree. Augustino: Great! Bye.
