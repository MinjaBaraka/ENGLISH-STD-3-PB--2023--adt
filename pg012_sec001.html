Activity 6: Listening to oral texts/observing signed video
(a) Listen to the short story/observe the signed video about Bahati’s room. Then, answer the following questions to help Bahati find her belongings.

Bahati’s room

Bahati’s room. Bahati’s room is messy. The school bag is on the bed. The shoes are under the bed. The red jacket is on the floor. The T-shirts are in the drawers. The juice bottle is on the chair. The pencil is on the Sofa. The yellow blouse is in the box. The radio is on the floor. Bahati is really a careless girl.

(i) Where is the school bag?
(ii) Where are the shoes?
(iii) Where is the yellow blouse?
(iv) Where is the pencil?
(v) Where are the T-shirts?
(b) Listen to the short story/Observe the signed video about The Proud Rose. Then, answer the following questions orally/sign.
(i) Why was the rose so proud itself?
(ii) Where did the rose and cactus grow?
(iii) Why did the rose complain?
(iv) What did other plants tell the rose?
