English Pupil’s Book Standard Three

Tutu: Your mum will be so excited.

Monde: Look, mum! I have caught a big fish.

Mother: How did you catch it, Monde?

Monde: Tutu came to help me pull it out of the water.

I couldn’t do it by myself.

Mother: You are a brave boy, Tutu!

Well, what are you going to do with the fish?

Monde: I’m going to cook it, mum.

I hope you will like it.

Mother: Certainly!
