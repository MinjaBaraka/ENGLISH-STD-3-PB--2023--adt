He pinned his hope on a nearby tree. He tried to climb the tree but failed. He tried again. He managed to climb up the tree. He sat on a big branch. He held it tight. The lions waited for him to fall. Finally, they went into the wild. Maswi waited a few more hours. He then climbed down from the tree. He went home and narrated the incident to his parents. They all said that he was lucky.
